Survive 60 Seconds – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026 · Developer: Bhushan Mohape

In short: the game has no accounts, no login and no game server. Your progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob uses to show optional rewarded ads.

This policy explains how the Android game Survive 60 Seconds (the "Game"), published by Bhushan Mohape ("I", "me"), handles information. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores the following only on your device, so it can remember your progress:

  • your best survival time and coin balance;
  • unlocked and selected characters;
  • achievement and daily-challenge progress;
  • settings (music, sound, vibration).

I do not receive, collect or have access to this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this save file in your personal Google backup, which is managed by Google under your account settings.

2. Information the Game does not collect

The Game itself does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, messages or precise (GPS) location, and it does not require any permissions to access them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads, for example to revive once or to double the coins earned in a run. Ads are never shown during gameplay, and watching one is always your choice.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads and the app;
  • diagnostic and crash information.

This data is sent over an encrypted connection and handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings → Privacy Choices.

5. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact me and it will be addressed.

6. Data deletion

Because the Game has no accounts or servers, I hold no personal data about you that can be deleted. To remove all game data from your device, uninstall the Game or clear its storage in Android settings. Advertising data is controlled through your Google account and device ad settings described above.

7. Security

Game progress is kept in the Game's private storage on your device. Data used by the advertising SDK is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
